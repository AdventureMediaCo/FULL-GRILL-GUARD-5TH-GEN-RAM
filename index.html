FULL GRILL GUARD 5TH GEN RAM

Looking to protect the entire front end of your truck? Then we have the solution for you! Our full grill guard system is available for all of our 2019-2024 Ram 2500/3500 Bumpers and it looks amazing. This particular showcase is shown on a 2020 Ram 2500 Octane front winch bumper- But its available for the both Octane & Attitude Series.

The Grill Guard system is 100% Modular and bolt on with your existing or new front Ram bumper. We designed the system to allow easy assembly, sandwiching between the outer wing and center section of the bumper, The grill guard system only requires drilling of two 3/8" holes for installation. Its constructed from CNC bent and laser cut 2" DOM Tube, along with 3/16" Steel uprights giving it the strength to keep your trucks front end undamaged.

The system also allows full view of your front camera, as well as no blockage of your headlights. Search in our catalog to see more!
